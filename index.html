About

I'm currently enrolled in a dual program, taking undergraduate and graduate classes at the same time! I plan to earn my B.S. in Mathematics with a Physics minor in the fall of 2026, and then my M.A. in the Spring of 2027.

Research

Applications of the Divergence Theorem to Gravitational Fields
Final Project Fall 2025
I used the divergence theorem to prove the Newton Shell theorem, and then used the results to calculate the gravity on a habitable planet like Earth, namely Kepler-452b.
Survey of Atmospheric Neutrinos
Final Project Spring 2026 (In progress)
Studied Atmospheric Neutrinos and some of the important detectors involved with the working of such particles. Provided applications and future directions for research of Atmospheric neutrinos.
